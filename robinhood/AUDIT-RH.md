# Audit log — GuardedSwapRH (Robinhood Chain)

**What is reviewed:** `robinhood/src/GuardedSwapRH.sol` — GuardedSwap v15 ported to the Robinhood Chain swap router `0x65050a9b7e5075a2ba5ced7b1b64ee66262c40dc` (chain 4663). Everything outside the route layer is v15 verbatim; its history is in `AUDIT.md` (rounds 1–14). Not deployed.

**Rule applied to every fix** (learned in the Dispatch cycle, where two of round 13's serious findings were consequences of earlier fixes): before a fix, ask who else can now pass the check and which invariant it weakens; prefer removing state or paths over adding them; one source of truth per concept.

## Round 1 (v1, commit `d81fd9e`) and what changed in v2

0 Critical, 1 High, 3 Medium, 5 Low, 6 Informational.

| ID | Severity | Finding | Disposition | What changed / why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-1 | High | `buy()` has no loss-budget check: (1) a stolen executor key can buy into its own pool every hour without an overall ceiling; (2) such buys inflate `costOf`, raising the 30% budget a later ruinous sale may use | Accepted as a contract residual; bounded at the system boundary | This is v15's documented residual (round 4 M-6, round 8 M-2, round 12 H-1): a buy has no price reference on chain for these tokens, so the contract cannot tell a bad buy from a thin market. The alternatives were weighed in round 12 and rejected for the same reasons here: booking a buy as a loss needs a price (an oracle or a self-declared one, removed in v8); excluding fresh buys from the budget's base blocks the honest exit of a position that crashes an hour after entry. What bounds it: (a) `MAX_BUY_ETH_PER_HOUR` per account (proposed 0.05 ETH, down from 0.1 in the v1 notes); (b) the overall ceiling the finding asks for lives in the delegation that admits the executor to the gate — a `nativeTokenPeriodTransfer` caveat, as on Dispatch (0.5 ETH / 24 h there; proposed 0.15 ETH / 24 h here); (c) every buy is a `Bought` event the watchers reconcile against the engine's journal, and the first foreign one pauses the gate, which since v14 stops buys and sales alike. Mechanism (2) is round 12 H-1 exactly; its increments are bounded by (a)–(c). DESIGN-RH.md states these numbers. |
| F-2 | Medium | For a v3 hop the gate checks `fee` against the factory but forwards `tickSpacing` unchecked; if the router keyed its callback on `tickSpacing` the two could diverge | Fixed | Resolved on the live router first (probe `test_P17_v3_tickSpacing_field`): its v3 callback check keys on `fee` alone — any `tickSpacing` (0, 1, 60, 200, −7) passes on both a fee-keyed Uniswap pool and a tick-keyed CL pool, every wrong `fee` fails `CallbackValidation`. v2 sends `tickSpacing = 0` and `hooks = 0` on every v3 hop, so no unchecked v3 field reaches the router. Mirror: nothing that passed before is refused (the router ignores the field), nothing new passes. Tests: `test_v3_tick_spacing_never_reaches_the_router` (fails on v1), `test_fork_v3_tick_spacing_is_not_forwarded`. |
| F-3 | Medium | `replaceGuardian` does not lift a pause the fired guardian set | Accepted / documented (as v15 round 14 I-2) | Mirror of the suggested fix: a pause is the watchers' answer to an executor-key theft. If replacing the guardian also lifted the pause, any guardian rotation during such an incident would silently resume the thief's trading, and the cold key would gain a second power over the pause it deliberately does not have (round 13 F-3 gave it replacement only). The new guardian's first transaction lifts the pause; `exit()` is never paused, so evacuation is unaffected throughout. Recorded as a runbook step. |
| F-4 | Medium | The post-sale `approve(0)` is best-effort: if the router took less than approved and the token refuses `approve(0)`, an allowance to the (upgradeable) router survives | Fixed | A sale must now consume exactly what the gate received: after the router call the gate's balance of the token is back at its base, else `PartialFill`. A standard token's exact allowance is then spent in full whatever `approve(0)` does. Chosen over the suggested `allowance() == 0` check because `allowance()` is the unreliable read (round 5 I-4): a token whose `allowance()` does not answer would brick `sell()` and `exit()` for that token. The sale-side leftover sweep is removed with it (nothing can come back), so v15 round 11 M-1 cannot arise. Mirror: stricter only; the liveness cost is that a partially filled route (thin liquidity) reverts instead of returning the rest — the engine and the exit page size the sale to the route. Tests: `test_partial_fill_refused_and_no_allowance_survives`, `test_fee_token_partial_route_refused` (both fail on v1), the invariant handler now mixes partial fills in. |
| L-1 | Low | No WETH-residue check on the buy path | Fixed | The check moved into `_callRouter`, the single router call site, so buys and sales share it (one place instead of two). Test: `test_weth_left_on_a_buy_refused` (fails on v1). |
| L-2 | Low | `token` is not checked against the router, the PoolManager or the factories | Accepted | Verified inert: none of the five answers `balanceOf(address)` (each reverts on chain), so a buy of any of them reverts at the gate's own balance read and a sale finds no lot. Adding a list check would add code for no reachable state. |
| L-3 | Low | The constructor cross-checks WETH and the PoolManager against the router, not the factory list | Accepted | The router exposes getters only for its Uniswap and Pancake factories, not for the CL factories its callback accepts, so there is nothing on chain to compare with. Deploy-time evidence is a fork round trip per configured factory (`test_fork_tick_keyed_factory_round_trip`, `test_fork_second_tick_keyed_factory_hop`, `test_fork_v3_single_hop_round_trip`). A factory the router does not accept can only make its routes revert in the router's callback; it never lets value through. |
| L-4 | Low | `_trim` keeps the last lot when the account's balance cannot be read | Accepted (v15 round 11 H-1) | Deliberate: an unreadable balance must not block `sell()` or `exit()`. |
| L-5 | Low | Fixed hourly windows allow a burst across a boundary | Accepted (v15 round 1 L-1) | Documented; the delegation's 24-hour cap bounds the total. |
| I-1 | Info | `_sweepToken`'s fallback counts what left the gate when the account's balance does not answer | Accepted (v15 round 13 L-3) | Now on the buy path only (the sale-side sweep is gone, F-4). |
| I-2 | Info | A rebasing token can close a lot without a budget check | Accepted (v15) | Rebasing tokens are reconciled by the lot rule; unsupported as before. |
| I-3 | Info | `_settle` uses `amount`, not the measured `got` | By design (v15 round 7 M-3, round 11 L-2) | The lot shrinks by what left the account; pull-side fees count as given up. |
| I-4 | Info | `maxSellsPerHour == 2^64−1` would overflow to a panic | Accepted | Needs a misconfigured deployment; production value 24. |
| I-5 | Info | After a pause expires the guardian cannot re-pause for a day | Accepted (v15 round 5 H-1) | The cooldown is what stops a hostile guardian from holding the executor forever; the cold key can replace the guardian at any time. |
| I-6 | Info | Recipient-zero and the PoolManager pin read as guarantees in the comments | Fixed (documentation) | NatSpec now says: recipient zero is how the live router pays the gate, and every output is also measured against `minOut`; the PoolManager written into v4 hops is canonical encoding only (the router uses its own, equal by the constructor check). |
