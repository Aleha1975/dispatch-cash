How it works

Non-custodial by design

01 · CONNECT

Your own smart account

Two signatures, zero transactions. A personal smart account is deployed for you; funds never leave an address only you own. We get a narrow, audited permission: manage LP in whitelisted pools — nothing else.

02 · EARN

Range + hedge engine

The keeper keeps liquidity concentrated around price (volatility-scaled width) and holds a matching HYPE short on Hyperliquid, re-hedged when delta drifts past 10%. You collect swap fees with little price exposure.

03 · WITHDRAW

One signature out

Exit any time to USDC in your account. Hedge margin is returned by us. Guardian pause, timelocked upgrades and on-chain price anchors protect against a compromised keeper.
