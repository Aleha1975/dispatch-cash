# Gate v18 (GuardedGate) - design, 09.11.2026 (reworked after the owner's decisions of 09.11; adversarial-review fixes applied the same day, section 4). **Round 4 (10.11, section 7) REMOVED the price reference: wherever sections 1-6 speak of `ref`, the 70% reference floor, `_refNote` or `floorFor` with a reference, they describe history; section 7 is current. Round 5 (section 8) replaced the loss record (plain leaky bucket) and the pause rules: statements about the loss record, `end` or pause episodes in sections 2 and 7.5-7.7 are history; section 8 is current.**

Goal: a client who already holds HYPE on Elysium starts with exactly 3 wallet prompts: TWO `eth_signTypedData` (the gate delegation, with the sign-in folded into it, and the `approve` delegation) and ONE funding transfer. Exit (cold key) permissions stay optional and off the start path. Local only: nothing deployed.

## 1. Decisions (owner, 09.11, final)

### 1.1 No custody
Bought tokens stay in the client's own smart account, as in the live v17.2 gate (`engine-elysium/test/contracts/src/GuardedSwap.sol`). A sale pulls them from the account with `transferFrom`, so the account must have approved the gate: the client signs a SECOND delegation, exactly the engine's existing `approve` kind (`clone/exec.mjs buildDelegation`): target any token, method `approve` (0x095ea7b3), spender pinned to the gate by `allowedCalldata` at calldata word 0, `valueLte 0`, expiry. The custody ledger, `held / owed / claim / release / pro-rata / Insolvent` logic and ERC-20 withdrawal through the gate are gone; the pooled-custody risk of the first v18 draft no longer exists. The client can always move his tokens from his own wallet. The lot (`basis`), the reconciliation against what the account holds (`_trim`, `_trimAll`, `reconcile`, 32-lot registry) and the sweep of bought tokens into the account are v17.2's, unchanged (the owner can move tokens out, so the lot must follow the account's balance).

Onboarding = signature 1 (gate delegation) + signature 2 (approve delegation) + 1 funding transfer. Selling to HYPE is the exit for tokens; there is no engine path that withdraws tokens.

### 1.2 Gate delegation: no value limits in the delegation, all limits in the gate
Delegation = delegate: executor key; caveats: `allowedTargets [gate]`, `allowedMethods {buy, sell, payFee, refundGas, withdraw}` (0x9e2044fc, 0xccda6635, 0x29610252, 0x4f6d3aed, 0x3ccfd60b; buy and sell changed in round 4, the list in section 7.9 is the only one to use), `timestamp` 90 days, `limitedCalls` 100000. **No `valueLte`, no `nativeTokenPeriodTransfer`.** (The kit's `FunctionCall` scope adds `valueLte 0` when none is given, so the caveats are built directly, as the engine already does for `approve`; the e2e asserts that no value enforcer is present.)

Every native outflow of the account through this delegation is pinned in the gate:
| Path | Where the value goes | Limit (in the gate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `buy{value}` | ROUTER, with the route decoded and pinned; change and kickbacks back to the account | per account 17.5 HYPE per sliding hour (two weighted buckets, immutable) |
| `payFee{value}` | `FEE_TO` (immutable) | 20% of the account's balance per call and per sliding 30 days (1.3) |
| `refundGas{value}` | `FEE_TO` | 0.02 per call, 0.1 per account per sliding day (immutable), and the day total also <= 5% of the balance |
| `withdraw{value}` | `owner()` of the calling account (a plain wallet/7702 account: itself) | none; never paused |
| `sell` | proceeds go to the account (`dstReceiver` = gate, forwarded to msg.sender after settlement) | loss budget on native + the sold lot's cost (round 4; no price reference) |
`receive()` accepts native only inside a call; the gate never keeps HYPE. Buy is the only path where native leaves the account towards anything but `owner()` / `FEE_TO`, and there it goes to the router and comes back as tokens or change.

**Stolen executor key with no delegation value cap.** What it can do through the gate delegation: (a) buy attacker-chosen tokens, at most 17.5 HYPE per sliding hour per account (two weighted buckets: no edge burst) - so up to 420 HYPE per day on a rich account until the guardian pauses (the watcher pauses on the first foreign trade) or the account is empty; with minOut > 0 and a pinned executor the attacker can only lose the account value into tokens he made liquid, not take HYPE directly; (b) sell lots through a rigged route: proceeds still go to the account, the loss below cost is capped by the 30% / 7-day budget measured on native + the sold lot's cost; a profit leak on an appreciated lot is NOT bounded on-chain (residual, section 7.2); (c) pay itself nothing: fee/gas go to FEE_TO only, at most 20% of the balance per sliding 30 days and 0.1 HYPE per day (<= 5% of the balance); (d) withdraw: only to the owner's own wallet, any amount - this is a denial of service (the capital sits in the owner's wallet instead of earning), not a theft; (e) approve delegation: approve to the gate only, no recipient. It cannot: send native to any address, raise a cap, reach `withdrawTo`, `forgetLot`, `exitSell`, `unlock`, `exit`. The removed delegation cap (the old 50 HYPE / 24 h) is therefore not the thing that bounds a thief any more: the bound is the hourly buy cap + the budget + the pause. Number to give the owner: 17.5 HYPE/h per account, nothing else leaves. Removing the delegation cap does not widen (a) because the previous 24 h cap (50) was above 17.5 x 2.9 h anyway for an active thief; it does remove the daily ceiling (50 -> 420), flagged below as a decision for the owner if he wants a daily buy cap in the gate (one more immutable and a 24 h window, cheap to add).

### 1.3 Fee: 20% of the balance, per call and per sliding 30 days
`payFee{value: x}`: before the transfer the account held `before = msg.sender.balance + msg.value` (the value has already left the account when the gate runs). Two checks:
1. `x <= 20% of before`;
2. `feeSum + x <= 20% of (before + feeSum + wdSum)` where, in the sliding 30-day window of the account, `feeSum` = fees already paid and `wdSum` = native sent by `withdrawTo` to an address that is neither the account nor its owner. **`withdraw()` (to the owner) credits nothing** (adversarial finding F6: the executor could call it in a loop with owner re-funding and widen the aggregate beyond 20%).
`before + feeSum + wdSum` is the money the account had at the start of the window plus deposits and sale proceeds (fees and withdrawals leave, and are added back), so with no deposits the total fees of a window cannot exceed 20% of the opening balance however many 20% calls a thief makes (a naive "20% of the current balance" compounds to 1 - 0.8^n). Fuzz test: random fee attempts, deposits and withdrawals in one window never exceed 20% of (opening + deposits). The window is a ring of sub-buckets (section 6.2; the two-bucket wording of 4.5 is history): every fee of the last 30 to 35 days counts in full, so there is no 2x edge burst. Consequence of F6: after a big withdrawal to the owner the aggregate is not widened, so a fee on a following profit may be only partly payable at once (test: opening 100, fee 6, withdraw 90, second profit 40: owed 6, `feeRoom` 4). The engine charges `min(owed, gate.feeRoom(account))` now and the rest later (the room grows again as the window slides); it must not advance the high-water mark by the unpaid part. Charge right after a profit is realised and BEFORE any withdrawal. `feeRoom(account)` is a view with the same two limits. Gas refund keeps its code caps (0.02 per call, 0.1 per account per day). Pause stops fee and gas, never withdraw.

### 1.4 Sells: no count cap, no proceeds cap; executor sell = budget only (round 4: the price reference is gone); the client's `exitSell` is outside it
Options (A) keep 24 sells/h and 100 HYPE/h, (B) drop both caps: **B chosen** (an honest sale is never refused for count or size; the cost of spam is the executor's gas).

**Executor `sell()`**: stays blocked while paused; keeps the loss budget (30% of native balance + cost of lots, 7-day linear refill, cold-key `unlock`); (round 4: the price reference of rounds 1-3 was removed, section 7.1).

**Client `exitSell(token, amount, minOut, routerData)`** (same arguments as `sell`): NOT in the delegation's AllowedMethods; requires `tx.origin == ownerOf(msg.sender)` (the same kind of proof as `exit()`'s `tx.origin == EXIT_KEY`); skips the loss budget and the price reference, books no loss; works while paused; still checks route, lot and minOut. The replaced `setExitMode` / `exitUntil` (a 3-day switch that let a thief dump every lot for dust once the owner set it, F1; it also did not help against pause, F5) is deleted. The executor can never call `exitSell`: a delegation to the executor key for it would fail on `tx.origin` (e2e). How the owner reaches it: his wallet signs (when he wants to exit, one signature) a delegation `account -> owner EOA`, caveats target = gate, method `exitSell`, expiry, and redeems it from his EOA in his own transaction (the real Hybrid account's `execute` accepts only the EntryPoint, itself and the DelegationManager, so the owner's EOA cannot call the gate "as the account" any other way; e2e proves this path while paused). With ERC-4337 the bundler is `tx.origin`, so a UserOp does not qualify. A plain / 7702 account is its own owner: it calls `exitSell` from its own EOA. Other ways out unchanged: the cold key's optional `exit()`, selling from the owner's wallet.

### 1.5 Kept unchanged from v17.2
Executor pin and decoded route (`_checkRoute`: pinned EXECUTOR, flags, no permit/fees, minOut >= caller's, buys pay the gate or the account, sales pay the gate), minOut > 0, only-the-lot rule with trimming, loss budget + cold-key `unlock`, guardian pause (<= 7 d, 1 d cooldown after a pause that ran to expiry, round 4), two-step guardian rotation + cold-key `replaceGuardian`, `exit()` via `tx.origin` (and now `exitSell()` via `tx.origin == owner`), bounded `balanceOf` (100k gas, floor 102k) now also used for the gate's own balance, `lock` reentrancy guard on every external entry, state final before any native or token leaves. L3 (bounded return data) is applied to every token call: `transfer`, `transferFrom` and `approve` are assembly calls copying <= 32 bytes; no return data = success, anything else must be one word == 1. solc 0.8.26 (pragma pinned, round 4), cancun, optimizer 10000 runs; runtime 20,658 bytes (round 4); no TSTORE/BLOBHASH/SELFDESTRUCT.

`owner()` read for `withdraw`: 100k gas, 32 bytes; `withdrawTo(address)` (account-only, not in the delegation) is the fallback when owner() cannot be resolved (contract-wallet owner, passkey-only, reverting): the owner, acting through his own account, names the recipient (not 0, gate, router).

## 4. Adversarial-review fixes (09.11, `test/Adversarial.t.sol`: each `test_VULN_*` inverted into `test_FIXED_*`)
1. **F1/F3/F5 exit mode** -> removed; `exitSell` (1.4). F3 (a thief burns the budget on a fake lot so the honest stop-loss is refused for a week) is not prevented for the executor, but no longer blocks the client: `exitSell` ignores the budget. F5: pause blocks the executor's `sell` only.
2. **F2 profit leak / price reference (reworked in round 2, section 5; REMOVED in round 4, section 7.1 - kept here as history).** Per-ACCOUNT, per-token `ref[account][token] = (price, at)`, price = implied ETH per 1e30 raw token units (uint256). Every executor buy (`spent/out`) and every executor sell (`out/sold`) of the account itself, of at least `MIN_REF_ETH` = 0.001 HYPE, raises that account's reference as a high-water mark; it fades linearly to zero over `REF_DECAY` = 2 days; one trade lifts it by at most 1.5x of the FADED live value (a single rigged buy cannot jump it); with no live reference the first note is at most 1.5x the lot's cost-basis price; a lower price changes nothing (no storage write). An executor sell with `out < ref_now * sold * 70%` books `max(cost - out, 70% * ref * sold - out)` as loss against the budget (or reverts `BudgetExceeded`). `exit()` and `exitSell()` are exempt and do not update it. Gas: one SLOAD, rarely one SSTORE.
   **Residual risk (honest):** (a) the reference only knows prices our own executor traded in the last 2 days; a token nobody traded, or a quiet 2+ days, has no reference and the thief is back to L4; (b) within the reference, a sale down to 70% of it is free of any booking, and beyond that the thief can leak until the 30% budget is used (per account and week): the bound is roughly `30% x ref value + budget`, not zero; (c) a thief can wait for the fade (the floor halves in 1 day); (d) the reference is a high-water mark, so a real fast crash (> 30% within 2 days) makes honest executor sells book a loss against the budget and may be refused - the client then uses `exitSell`; (e) it can be poisoned upwards only by the account's own executor key, at the price of real money: each step is at most 1.5x of the faded value, needs a real buy/sell with >= 0.001 HYPE and spends that account's hourly 17.5 buy cap; the effect is refused/booked executor sells, never a loss of funds (round 1 wrongly assumed this also covered third parties; it did not, see section 5.1); (f) the price is per raw unit, so it is meaningful only for the token's own history (that is all it is used for). It is a speed bump, not an oracle.
3. **F4 registry poisoning.** `forgetLot(token)`: account-only, not delegated, deletes the lot (cost leaves the basis) without any `balanceOf`; `MIN_NEW_LOT` = 0.001 HYPE of spend for a new lot (`DustLot`); `_trimAll` stops after `TRIM_GAS` = 600k gas and a per-account cursor resumes the walk on the next call (test: 32 gas-burning lots add < 0.9M gas per sale instead of +2.8M; after the owner forgets them a new buy works). Residual: with the registry full of junk the executor's copy-trade entries of new tokens fail until the owner calls `forgetLot` (an UI "clean up" action); the junk cost the victim real HYPE (each >= 0.001, hourly cap 17.5). Until the walk completes the budget base may include cost of lots the owner moved out (budget slightly larger, never smaller).
4. **F6** -> `withdraw()` no longer credits `wdSum`; only `withdrawTo` to a non-owner non-self address does (1.3).
5. **F7 sliding windows.** (Replaced in round 3 by a ring of sub-buckets, section 6.) Fees (30 d), buys (1 h), gas (1 d): two aligned buckets; total = current + previous * (share of the current slot still to run). Edge bursts are gone; round 2 (L-1) corrected this wording: the first version under-counted spend concentrated at the end of two adjacent slots (up to 2x the limit inside one span, not "< 1 wei" of slack). The total is now `max(cur + prev * w, prev)` (w = share of the current slot still to run), so one span can never carry more than the limit; the price is that after a full-cap spend in slot k nothing more is allowed until the previous bucket stops counting in full, i.e. up to two slots (the sustained rate stays at most 17.5/h; a burst needs an idle slot). Withdrawn amounts in the fee base keep the plain weighting (under-counting them is the safe side).
6. **refundGas** day total also <= 5% of (balance before the call) per account.
7. **MetaMask HybridDeleGator interface check.** The Solidity source of the delegation framework is not in this repo (`lib/` has only forge-std); the published ABI (`@metamask/delegation-abis` 2.0.0, `HybridDeleGator`) was inspected: `owner() view returns (address)` (the EOA signer; Ownable: `transferOwnership`, `renounceOwnership`), `NAME()`, `getKey`, `getKeyIdHashes` (passkeys), `execute(...)` (EntryPoint/self only), `executeFromExecutor(bytes32,bytes)` (DelegationManager). So `ownerOf` reading `owner()` is the right selector 0x8da5cb5b with an address return; the e2e on the real deployed Hybrid account confirms the gate reads the client's wallet. Caveats: `owner()` is `address(0)` after `renounceOwnership` and may be a non-EOA after `transferOwnership` (the gate then reverts `NoBeneficiary`: withdraw fails safe, `withdrawTo` is the fallback); a passkey-only signer does not appear in `owner()`.

## 5. Round 2: fixes for external audit job 1205 (its reference items H-1, L-2, L-3, L-4 and M-1 are history after round 4) (report `audit_1205.md`; tests in `test/Round2.t.sol`)

The first note for a lot is the account's own buy price; the step cap uses the FADED value (`_refPrice * 1.5`), never the stored undecayed price; a note with no live reference may not exceed 1.5x the lot's cost-basis price (`ethIn / tokens`). An attacker can still poison its own account; that is its own loss. ## 6. Round 3: fixes for external audit job 1206 (report `audit_1206.md`; tests in `test/Round3.t.sol`; the reference items 1, 3, 4, 5 are history after round 4, the ring windows of item 2 stay)
1. **M-1 reference ratchet.** The 0.001 HYPE dust notes and the 1.5x step per call let one transaction ratchet the reference ~3,300x and either block executor sells or book a phantom loss. Now (`_refNote`, `_refPrice`): (a) a note needs `eth >= max(0.001 HYPE, 5% of the lot's ethIn)` (sell: the lot before the sale; buy: the lot before the buy); (b) at most one raise per account+token per 10 minutes (`REF_GAP`, compares `ref.at`; later notes inside it are ignored; lowering is not needed, the reference is a high-water mark that fades); (c) while a lot exists the reference is capped at 3x the lot's cost-basis price, when noting and again when read. Tests reproduce 20 dust buys in one instant (reference unchanged, fair sale books no loss), 20 qualifying rigged buys (one 1.5x step, <= 3x cost), a 12-step ramp (always <= 3x cost) and the phantom variant (booked <= 1.1x the lot's cost instead of 3,300x). Residual: an executor sale at cost can still book up to 1.1x of the lot's cost against the budget after a deliberate ramp (3 notes of 10 minutes, each a real buy of >= 5% of the lot); `exitSell` is unaffected. The section 4.2(e) claim ("spends the hourly cap") is replaced by this text.
2. **L-1 window lock-out.** Buys (10 min slots), gas (4 h) and fees (5 days) use a ring of 6 sub-buckets per span plus the running slot (7 slots; with 6 only, a spend at the end of a slot would leave the count 1/6 span early and over-allow). The full sum is counted: a spend stays counted for at least one span and at most span + 1/6 span. After a full-cap spend the next buy is possible after at most 70 minutes (refused at 59). Best sustained rate: a full burst of 17.5 every 70 minutes (15 HYPE/h) or 2.5 every 10 minutes; any 60-minute interval carries at most 17.5 (property test). Backward time: the stored head is kept (`max(now / slot, head)`), nothing resets; if the head jumped more than 6 slots ahead before the clock returned, older slots are already cleared (the same as the old bucket jump of 2+ slots). Withdrawn amounts in the fee base sum 6 slots only (under-counting is the safe side). Storage: `Ring` (head + 7 x uint128), `pay` and `window` getters replaced by `windows(account)` returning the four totals. Gas: a buy reads 5 slots more than before.
3. **L-2.** `ref[account][token]` is deleted in `_closeLot` (full sale, full exitSell / exit, forgetLot, reconcile-close) and when a fresh lot opens in `buy`. Test: the auditor's honest re-entry sequence (spike note, full sale, re-entry at P/2, sale at cost: no loss, no revert).
4. **L-3.** `floorFor` = max(cost floor, 70% of the faded reference) less the free budget room, the same helper (`_refFloor`) as `_settle`; tests: a sale at the floor passes, just below it reverts `BudgetExceeded`. It remains a view for an executor sale (exitSell has no floor).
5. **L-4** (reference precision at extreme price ratios): no change; the reference is soft and fails toward not blocking.
6. **I-1** docs fixed: 687 lines, runtime 23,234 bytes, buy bound 17.5 per hour-long span (15/h as a sustained burst rate), not 420/day as a guarantee. **I-2** (`_call3` rejects tokens returning more than 32 bytes: fail-safe, exotic tokens only) and **I-4** (`withdrawTo` round trip widens the account's own fee base: self-inflicted, account-only, not delegated) accepted. **I-3** (`tx.origin`, owner contract wallets use `withdrawTo`) documented.
7. Unchanged and re-checked: `withdraw()` of the full balance to the owner even when paused; `exitSell` / `exit` never capped; no count / fraction / proceeds cap on executor sells; fee <= 20% per call and per 30 days; buys <= 17.5 HYPE per hour-long span. `forge test`: 86 tests pass; e2e on the local anvil fork: 35 checks, ALL PASSED.

## 7. Round 4: fixes for external audit job 1208 (report `audit_1208.md`; tests in `test/Round4.t.sol`)
Decision of the owner (final): the price reference is removed entirely; the other findings are fixed or documented as below. Contract 680 lines, runtime 20,658 bytes (limit 24,576), pragma pinned to 0.8.26. `forge test`: 85 tests pass; e2e on the local anvil fork: 37 checks, ALL PASSED.

### 7.1 Price reference removed (closes M-1, L-2, L-3, I-10 of 1208; and I-3 partly)
Gone: `ref` mapping and getter, `_refNote`, `_refPrice`, `_refFloor`, the reference floor in `_settle` and `floorFor`, the constants `MAX_DROP_BPS`, `REF_*`, `MIN_REF_ETH`, `PRICE_SCALE`, the deletes in `_closeLot` / `buy`. Three audit rounds found new attack surface in it each time for a bound of about 1.1x the lot's cost. M-1 (re-basing), L-2 (step cap anchor), L-3 (`floorFor(.., 0)` division by zero) and I-10 (stated bound, `unlock` not clearing `ref`) are therefore moot. An executor sale is bounded below cost by the loss budget only.

### 7.2 Residual: profit leak through a rigged route (stolen executor key)
A sale of an appreciated lot through a route the thief shapes (`srcReceivers` chosen by the route data) can divert the profit above cost: the gate sees only `out >= cost - budget`. Not closed on-chain (business rule: an executor sale may exit a whole position at once, no count / fraction / proceeds cap). Mitigation off-chain: the watcher compares the price of every executor sale with the market and the guardian pauses executor sells (`setPaused`); `exitSell` and `withdraw` are not affected by the pause. Bounded below cost by the 30% budget; bounded above by the value of the lots the thief can sell.

### 7.3 M-2 budget base (option A)
`_saleBase(account, lotEth) = account.balance + basis[account][token].ethIn`: the budget of an executor sale is 30% of the native balance plus the cost of the lot under sale. Cost of other lots gives no room, so the 12-cycle junk-buy sequence (buy a token of the attacker, native comes back after the call, `costOf` +17.5 each time) no longer helps: `test_M2_twelve_junk_buy_cycles_do_not_let_a_rigged_sale_of_lot_R_through` runs the sequence (costOf 245, old budget 80) and the sale of R reverts `BudgetExceeded` (budget 17.25). `floorFor` uses the same base and is exact at the edge (`test_M2_floorFor_...`); `budgetOf(account, token)` now takes the token. `costOf` stays as bookkeeping; no limit reads it. Consequence: a client with several lots has less room per sale than before (30% of native + that lot).

### 7.4 L-5 (cost of an emptied lot staying in the base): moot
The base no longer includes other lots, and the sold token's lot is always reconciled (`_trim`) before the base is read. `_trimAll` still runs in a sale, but only to keep the registry and `costOf` true. Test `test_L5_cost_of_other_lots_gives_no_room`.

### 7.5 L-1 pause cooldown
Cooldown now follows only a pause that RAN TO ITS EXPIRY (or was lifted after it expired). An early lift (guardian) sets `pauseEnd = 0`, so the next pause is possible at once. To keep the protection against a hostile guardian that pauses, lifts and pauses again, the time used by early-lifted pauses is added up per episode (`pauseUsed`, `episodeAt`): a pause may last `PAUSE_MAX - pauseUsed`, so the total of an episode never exceeds 7 days; the episode (and `pauseUsed`) resets 8 days after it began or after a cooldown. `replaceGuardian` (cold key) resets cooldown and used time (a running pause stays), so a replacement guardian inherits nothing; `acceptGuardian` (guardian rotating to itself) deliberately does not reset. Tests: `test_L1_*` (six).

### 7.6 L-4 `_trimAll` wraps
If a call resumed in the middle of a round and reached index 0 with gas left, it continues once from the end of the registry down to where it started, so a slot freed above the cursor is found in the same call (and the buy that needed it no longer reverts `TooManyLots` again and again). A finished round stores cursor 0. The auditor's example (cursor below the emptied lot) is `test_L4_trim_wraps_so_a_freed_slot_above_the_cursor_is_found`; with the wrap disabled the test fails with `TooManyLots`. Under an adversarial registry of gas-burning lots the walk is still bounded by `TRIM_GAS`; the engine should call `reconcile(account)` on `TooManyLots`, the owner can `forgetLot`.

### 7.7 L-6 loss record
`Loss{amount, at, end}`: one running accumulator. A new booking adds to the outstanding amount and sets `end` to the amount-weighted average of the old remaining time and a full 7 days for the new part (rounded up); the record fades linearly from `amount` at `at` to zero at `end`. A 1-wei booking moves `end` by at most one second; the area under the curve (loss-seconds) is preserved. Tests: `test_L6_one_wei_loss_does_not_restart_the_refill`, `test_L6_new_loss_adds_and_fades_between_the_two_clocks`. The `loss(address)` getter now returns three values.

### 8.2 L-1 + L-3 + I-1 pause window
One rule: in a window of 8 days (`PAUSE_MAX + PAUSE_COOLDOWN`) the pauses add up to at most 7 days. A new pause needs `pauseUsed < 7 d` and lasts the remaining allowance. ### 9.1 Why the loss record produced a Medium three rounds in a row (analysis before the patch)
Round 3 (accumulator with `end`), round 4 (weighted end, then M-2 base), round 5 (leaky bucket in HYPE), round 5 re-audit (M-1, L-1). Every version stored the record in an ABSOLUTE amount (HYPE) while one account has MANY lots, and read it with a budget (cap and drain rate) taken from the lot under sale. So two values that must be properties of the account (how fast the record empties, how much it can hold) were properties of a lot, and the lot is chosen by the caller:
- the executor key can create lots of any cost at no net cost (buy through its own pool, take the native back later). A lot with a bigger cost has a bigger budget, so it drains the record faster (M-1) and holds more;
- a lot with a smaller base drains slower and caps lower than the base that booked the loss, so an honest stop-loss waits for weeks (L-1). Each patch moved the leak (cap in round 4, drain rate in round 5) without removing the dependency.
Patching again (for example "drain at the smaller of the stored and the current budget", option 2 of the report) makes L-1 worse and keeps a lot-dependent number in the formula. Per-token records (option 3) give every junk lot a new full record next to the native balance.

### 9.2 Chosen design: the record is a fraction of one budget
`Loss{fill, at}`, `fill` in [0, ONE], ONE = 1e18 = one full budget. Drain: `ONE / 7 days` per second, for every sale. Cap: ONE. A booking of `added` on a sale with budget `B` adds `ceil(added * ONE / B)`. The sale is refused when the sum would pass ONE. Consequences: (1) the rate and the cap are constants, no lot (and no executor-created lot) can change them; (2) for any mix of lots the booked losses satisfy `sum(added_i / B_i) <= 1 + T / 7 days`, i.e. at most one budget plus one budget per week, where each loss is measured against the budget of the lot that booked it; (3) L-1 goes away by the same choice: a full record is at most 1.0 and empties in 7 days whatever base follows (a small lot's stop-loss books a larger share of a smaller budget, so it needs the record low enough, but never waits more than 7 days); (4) a deposit no longer speeds the drain retroactively (I-10). Conservative edges: a booking on a small base uses a larger share; a key holder can fill the record with a loss on a junk lot and so stop executor stop-losses for at most 7 days (already accepted F3; `unlock` by the cold key and `exitSell` / `exit` are unaffected). Rounding: bookings round up, the drain rounds down.
`loss(account)` returns `(fill, at)`. `budgetOf(account, token)` returns `(lost, budget)` with `lost = ceil(fill * budget / ONE)` (HYPE as a share of this lot's budget) and the same `budget` the sale uses. `floorFor` uses `room = budget * (ONE - fill) / ONE`, which is exactly what `_settle` accepts. `BudgetExceeded(lossAfter, budget)` still reports HYPE (`ceil(fill_after * budget / ONE)`).

### 9.3 L-2 + L-3 pause window: a sliding ring, replaceGuardian leaves it alone
The round-5 state (`pauseUsed`, `episodeAt`, window reset on "8 days and 1 day gap") was an episode, not a sliding window: `replaceGuardian` could reset it (chains of 13-14 days) and pauses spaced under 24 h never reset it (L-3). Now the seconds of pause are booked per day in a ring of 9 day-slots (`_pr`, `uint32[9]` + head). `pauseUsed()` = sum of the 9 slots that touch the last 8 days (the oldest day counted whole: never under-counts, so an honest guardian may wait up to one extra day). A new pause lasts `7 d - pauseUsed()` and is refused with `PauseCooldown` when that is 0. Soundness: any 8-day window that ends inside or after the new pause contains the new pause and only old pause seconds that were already inside the last 8 days at its start. `replaceGuardian` only books an expired never-lifted pause (and emits `Paused(false)`, I-4); a running pause keeps its start and end; it creates no allowance. `episodeAt` is removed; `pauseUsed()` stays as a view (same selector), `pauseEnd` stays (informational). Storage slots moved (`_trimPos` is now slot 17; tests only).
Honest guardian: 2 h of false alarm per day never locks it out (`test_L3_daily_false_alarms_never_use_up_the_allowance`: day 60 pause gets > 6 days). After a full 7-day pause the next pause is available gradually from about day 9 (a day of window per day), not "7 d at day 8" as in round 5.

### 9.4 L-4 free shave of one unit
An executor `sell` must release at least 1 wei of cost (`cost == 0` -> `ZeroAmount`). For 18-decimal tokens this stops one-unit shaves (a unit costs < 1 wei). A shave that releases cost with `out >= cost` books no loss and still lowers the lot: that residual remains (executor sells have no size rule by decision), so the exit procedure stays: the guardian pause stops `sell` at once (never `exitSell` / `exit`), and the site / cold-key tooling signs `lot - margin`, or `lot` after a pause. No on-chain "sell the whole lot" form: the router calldata must carry the exact amount pulled.

### 9.5 L-5 exits depend on the allowance (off-chain fix, no on-chain path exists)
The gate cannot move tokens without an allowance and the executor key can set it to 0 (the approve delegation pins the spender word, not the amount). Pinning the amount word is rejected: the engine sets exact allowances and a pin to `max` would be a second hidden policy. The exit paths re-approve themselves: the owner's `exitSell` and the cold key's `exit` are sent as ONE transaction redeeming two delegations (approve for exactly the exit amount, spender pinned to the gate; then `exitSell` / `exit`). The e2e proves it (executor `approve(gate, 0)`, `exitSell` alone fails, the two-delegation transaction succeeds); `test_L5_exit_after_the_executor_zeroed_the_allowance_needs_the_batched_approve` shows the same with a batching account. See ENGINE_CHANGES.md Round 6 for the delegations to add.

### 9.6 L-6 junk lots
Sells no longer walk the registry (it fed no limit since round 4): executor `sell` costs the same with 32 gas-burning lots (`test_L6_executor_sell_is_cheap_with_32_gas_burning_lots`, limit 400k). The walk stays in `reconcile` (guardian / cold key / account) and in `buy` on a full registry. `forgetLots(address[])` (account-only, not delegated, <= 32 tokens) clears junk lots in one owner transaction. Not done: letting the cold key forget a lot whose read does not answer (a closing power on someone's books without the owner; the owner has `forgetLots`).
