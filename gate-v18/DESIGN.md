# Gate v18 (GuardedGate) - design, 09.11.2026 (reworked after the owner's decisions of 09.11; adversarial-review fixes applied the same day, section 4)

Goal: a client who already holds HYPE on Elysium starts with exactly 3 wallet prompts: TWO `eth_signTypedData` (the gate delegation, with the sign-in folded into it, and the `approve` delegation) and ONE funding transfer. Exit (cold key) permissions stay optional and off the start path. Local only: nothing deployed.

## 1. Decisions (owner, 09.11, final)

### 1.1 No custody
Bought tokens stay in the client's own smart account, as in the live v17.2 gate (`engine-elysium/test/contracts/src/GuardedSwap.sol`). A sale pulls them from the account with `transferFrom`, so the account must have approved the gate: the client signs a SECOND delegation, exactly the engine's existing `approve` kind (`clone/exec.mjs buildDelegation`): target any token, method `approve` (0x095ea7b3), spender pinned to the gate by `allowedCalldata` at calldata word 0, `valueLte 0`, expiry. The custody ledger, `held / owed / claim / release / pro-rata / Insolvent` logic and ERC-20 withdrawal through the gate are gone; the pooled-custody risk of the first v18 draft no longer exists. The client can always move his tokens from his own wallet. The lot (`basis`), the reconciliation against what the account holds (`_trim`, `_trimAll`, `reconcile`, 32-lot registry) and the sweep of bought tokens into the account are v17.2's, unchanged (the owner can move tokens out, so the lot must follow the account's balance).

Onboarding = signature 1 (gate delegation) + signature 2 (approve delegation) + 1 funding transfer. Selling to HYPE is the exit for tokens; there is no engine path that withdraws tokens.

### 1.2 Gate delegation: no value limits in the delegation, all limits in the gate
Delegation = delegate: executor key; caveats: `allowedTargets [gate]`, `allowedMethods {buy, sell, payFee, refundGas, withdraw}` (0xf496a610, 0x3581f5bb, 0x29610252, 0x4f6d3aed, 0x3ccfd60b), `timestamp` 90 days, `limitedCalls` 100000. **No `valueLte`, no `nativeTokenPeriodTransfer`.** (The kit's `FunctionCall` scope adds `valueLte 0` when none is given, so the caveats are built directly, as the engine already does for `approve`; the e2e asserts that no value enforcer is present.)

Every native outflow of the account through this delegation is pinned in the gate:
| Path | Where the value goes | Limit (in the gate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `buy{value}` | ROUTER, with the route decoded and pinned; change and kickbacks back to the account | per account 17.5 HYPE per sliding hour (two weighted buckets, immutable) |
| `payFee{value}` | `FEE_TO` (immutable) | 20% of the account's balance per call and per sliding 30 days (1.3) |
| `refundGas{value}` | `FEE_TO` | 0.02 per call, 0.1 per account per sliding day (immutable), and the day total also <= 5% of the balance |
| `withdraw{value}` | `owner()` of the calling account (a plain wallet/7702 account: itself) | none; never paused |
| `sell` | proceeds go to the account (`dstReceiver` = gate, forwarded to msg.sender after settlement) | loss budget + per-token price reference (1.4) |
`receive()` accepts native only inside a call; the gate never keeps HYPE. Buy is the only path where native leaves the account towards anything but `owner()` / `FEE_TO`, and there it goes to the router and comes back as tokens or change.

**Stolen executor key with no delegation value cap.** What it can do through the gate delegation: (a) buy attacker-chosen tokens, at most 17.5 HYPE per sliding hour per account (two weighted buckets: no edge burst) - so up to 420 HYPE per day on a rich account until the guardian pauses (the watcher pauses on the first foreign trade) or the account is empty; with minOut > 0 and a pinned executor the attacker can only lose the account value into tokens he made liquid, not take HYPE directly; (b) sell lots through a rigged route: proceeds still go to the account, the loss below cost OR below 70% of the token's recent market reference is capped by the 30% / 7-day budget (L4, 1.4, residual in 4.2); (c) pay itself nothing: fee/gas go to FEE_TO only, at most 20% of the balance per sliding 30 days and 0.1 HYPE per day (<= 5% of the balance); (d) withdraw: only to the owner's own wallet, any amount - this is a denial of service (the capital sits in the owner's wallet instead of earning), not a theft; (e) approve delegation: approve to the gate only, no recipient. It cannot: send native to any address, raise a cap, reach `withdrawTo`, `forgetLot`, `exitSell`, `unlock`, `exit`. The removed delegation cap (the old 50 HYPE / 24 h) is therefore not the thing that bounds a thief any more: the bound is the hourly buy cap + the budget + the pause. Number to give the owner: 17.5 HYPE/h per account, nothing else leaves. Removing the delegation cap does not widen (a) because the previous 24 h cap (50) was above 17.5 x 2.9 h anyway for an active thief; it does remove the daily ceiling (50 -> 420), flagged below as a decision for the owner if he wants a daily buy cap in the gate (one more immutable and a 24 h window, cheap to add).

### 1.3 Fee: 20% of the balance, per call and per sliding 30 days
`payFee{value: x}`: before the transfer the account held `before = msg.sender.balance + msg.value` (the value has already left the account when the gate runs). Two checks:
1. `x <= 20% of before`;
2. `feeSum + x <= 20% of (before + feeSum + wdSum)` where, in the sliding 30-day window of the account, `feeSum` = fees already paid and `wdSum` = native sent by `withdrawTo` to an address that is neither the account nor its owner. **`withdraw()` (to the owner) credits nothing** (adversarial finding F6: the executor could call it in a loop with owner re-funding and widen the aggregate beyond 20%).
`before + feeSum + wdSum` is the money the account had at the start of the window plus deposits and sale proceeds (fees and withdrawals leave, and are added back), so with no deposits the total fees of a window cannot exceed 20% of the opening balance however many 20% calls a thief makes (a naive "20% of the current balance" compounds to 1 - 0.8^n). Fuzz test: random fee attempts, deposits and withdrawals in one window never exceed 20% of (opening + deposits). The window is a two-bucket weighted sliding window (4.7): fees of the previous 30-day slot count with the share of the current slot still to run, so there is no 2x edge burst. Consequence of F6: after a big withdrawal to the owner the aggregate is not widened, so a fee on a following profit may be only partly payable at once (test: opening 100, fee 6, withdraw 90, second profit 40: owed 6, `feeRoom` 4). The engine charges `min(owed, gate.feeRoom(account))` now and the rest later (the room grows again as the window slides); it must not advance the high-water mark by the unpaid part. Charge right after a profit is realised and BEFORE any withdrawal. `feeRoom(account)` is a view with the same two limits. Gas refund keeps its code caps (0.02 per call, 0.1 per account per day). Pause stops fee and gas, never withdraw.

### 1.4 Sells: no count cap, no proceeds cap; executor sell = budget + price reference; the client's `exitSell` is outside both
Options (A) keep 24 sells/h and 100 HYPE/h, (B) drop both caps: **B chosen** (an honest sale is never refused for count or size; the cost of spam is the executor's gas).

**Executor `sell()`**: stays blocked while paused; keeps the loss budget (30% of native balance + cost of lots, 7-day linear refill, cold-key `unlock`); and checks the **price reference** (4.2): an implied price (`out / sold`) below 70% of the token's reference books the shortfall as loss.

**Client `exitSell(token, amount, minOut, routerData)`** (same arguments as `sell`): NOT in the delegation's AllowedMethods; requires `tx.origin == ownerOf(msg.sender)` (the same kind of proof as `exit()`'s `tx.origin == EXIT_KEY`); skips the loss budget and the price reference, books no loss; works while paused; still checks route, lot and minOut. The replaced `setExitMode` / `exitUntil` (a 3-day switch that let a thief dump every lot for dust once the owner set it, F1; it also did not help against pause, F5) is deleted. The executor can never call `exitSell`: a delegation to the executor key for it would fail on `tx.origin` (e2e). How the owner reaches it: his wallet signs (when he wants to exit, one signature) a delegation `account -> owner EOA`, caveats target = gate, method `exitSell`, expiry, and redeems it from his EOA in his own transaction (the real Hybrid account's `execute` accepts only the EntryPoint, itself and the DelegationManager, so the owner's EOA cannot call the gate "as the account" any other way; e2e proves this path while paused). With ERC-4337 the bundler is `tx.origin`, so a UserOp does not qualify. A plain / 7702 account is its own owner: it calls `exitSell` from its own EOA. Other ways out unchanged: the cold key's optional `exit()`, selling from the owner's wallet.

### 1.5 Kept unchanged from v17.2
Executor pin and decoded route (`_checkRoute`: pinned EXECUTOR, flags, no permit/fees, minOut >= caller's, buys pay the gate or the account, sales pay the gate), minOut > 0, only-the-lot rule with trimming, loss budget + cold-key `unlock`, guardian pause (<= 7 d, 1 d cooldown), two-step guardian rotation + cold-key `replaceGuardian`, `exit()` via `tx.origin` (and now `exitSell()` via `tx.origin == owner`), bounded `balanceOf` (100k gas, floor 102k) now also used for the gate's own balance, `lock` reentrancy guard on every external entry, state final before any native or token leaves. L3 (bounded return data) is applied to every token call: `transfer`, `transferFrom` and `approve` are assembly calls copying <= 32 bytes; no return data = success, anything else must be one word == 1. solc 0.8.26, cancun, optimizer 10000 runs; runtime ~19.3 kB; no TSTORE/BLOBHASH/SELFDESTRUCT.

`owner()` read for `withdraw`: 100k gas, 32 bytes; `withdrawTo(address)` (account-only, not in the delegation) is the fallback when owner() cannot be resolved (contract-wallet owner, passkey-only, reverting): the owner, acting through his own account, names the recipient (not 0, gate, router).

## 2. Stolen executor key, per client account: today (live v17.2 + 5 delegations) vs v18
| | Today | v18 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy into attacker tokens | <= 8.7 HYPE/h; delegation 24 h cap up to ~580 | <= 17.5 HYPE/h, no daily ceiling in the delegation (up to 420/day until paused or the account is empty) |
| Sell lots (rigged route) | <= 29 HYPE/h, 12 sells/h; loss <= 30% per 7 d | no count/proceeds cap; loss below cost or below 70% of the token's price reference <= 30% per 7 d (same budget); profit leak bounded by the reference (4.2), residual stated there |
| Native out to an attacker address | none (withdraw pinned to owner) | none |
| Native withdrawn to the owner | up to the delegation cap (1,000,000 HYPE) | any amount, to owner() only (DoS only) |
| Fee/gas | `gas` delegation pays the executor key itself (<= 0.087 per 30 d); fee up to ~145 HYPE lifetime | nothing reaches the key; FEE_TO only: fee <= 20% of the balance per 30 days, gas <= 0.1/day |
| Approve | any token, spender pinned to the gate | same (unchanged) |
| Tokens | stay in the account | stay in the account (no pooled custody) |
| Pause | stops buy/sell | stops buy/sell/fee/gas; withdraw, exit, exitSell unaffected |
| Signatures at start | 5-7 | 2 (+ funding) |

Net vs today: the per-hour buy headroom doubles, the daily buy ceiling is removed from the delegation (the gate's hourly cap is the only bound), every payout recipient is code-pinned, the fee is bounded relative to the client's balance, and no ERC-20 sits in the gate. Residuals as v17.2: sale `srcReceivers` chosen by the aggregator, buys have no price reference, the budget is relative to cost (sells now also have the weak reference of 4.2).

## 3. Open points for the owner
1. Daily buy ceiling: none now (hourly 17.5 only). Add `MAX_BUY_ETH_PER_DAY` (e.g. 50) in the gate? It would reinstate the old delegation's 50/day bound without touching withdrawals.
2. L4 widened by dropping the sell caps (1.4); narrowed again by the price reference but not closed (4.2). Alternative (A) is a one-line immutable if wanted.
3. Fee 20% is a ceiling; the owner's 15% HWM rule is the engine's job.
4. exitSell reach: the owner needs `account -> owner EOA` delegation (1 signature at exit time) or a 7702 account; a smart account whose owner() is a contract wallet / passkey-only cannot prove `tx.origin` and keeps `withdrawTo` for cash, the cold key `exit()` and moving tokens from the wallet.

## 4. Adversarial-review fixes (09.11, `test/Adversarial.t.sol`: each `test_VULN_*` inverted into `test_FIXED_*`)
1. **F1/F3/F5 exit mode** -> removed; `exitSell` (1.4). F3 (a thief burns the budget on a fake lot so the honest stop-loss is refused for a week) is not prevented for the executor, but no longer blocks the client: `exitSell` ignores the budget. F5: pause blocks the executor's `sell` only.
2. **F2 profit leak / price reference (reworked in round 2, section 5).** Per-ACCOUNT, per-token `ref[account][token] = (price, at)`, price = implied ETH per 1e30 raw token units (uint256). Every executor buy (`spent/out`) and every executor sell (`out/sold`) of the account itself, of at least `MIN_REF_ETH` = 0.001 HYPE, raises that account's reference as a high-water mark; it fades linearly to zero over `REF_DECAY` = 2 days; one trade lifts it by at most 1.5x of the FADED live value (a single rigged buy cannot jump it); with no live reference the first note is at most 1.5x the lot's cost-basis price; a lower price changes nothing (no storage write). An executor sell with `out < ref_now * sold * 70%` books `max(cost - out, 70% * ref * sold - out)` as loss against the budget (or reverts `BudgetExceeded`). `exit()` and `exitSell()` are exempt and do not update it. Gas: one SLOAD, rarely one SSTORE.
   **Residual risk (honest):** (a) the reference only knows prices our own executor traded in the last 2 days; a token nobody traded, or a quiet 2+ days, has no reference and the thief is back to L4; (b) within the reference, a sale down to 70% of it is free of any booking, and beyond that the thief can leak until the 30% budget is used (per account and week): the bound is roughly `30% x ref value + budget`, not zero; (c) a thief can wait for the fade (the floor halves in 1 day); (d) the reference is a high-water mark, so a real fast crash (> 30% within 2 days) makes honest executor sells book a loss against the budget and may be refused - the client then uses `exitSell`; (e) it can be poisoned upwards only by the account's own executor key, at the price of real money: each step is at most 1.5x of the faded value, needs a real buy/sell with >= 0.001 HYPE and spends that account's hourly 17.5 buy cap; the effect is refused/booked executor sells, never a loss of funds (round 1 wrongly assumed this also covered third parties; it did not, see section 5.1); (f) the price is per raw unit, so it is meaningful only for the token's own history (that is all it is used for). It is a speed bump, not an oracle.
3. **F4 registry poisoning.** `forgetLot(token)`: account-only, not delegated, deletes the lot (cost leaves the basis) without any `balanceOf`; `MIN_NEW_LOT` = 0.001 HYPE of spend for a new lot (`DustLot`); `_trimAll` stops after `TRIM_GAS` = 600k gas and a per-account cursor resumes the walk on the next call (test: 32 gas-burning lots add < 0.9M gas per sale instead of +2.8M; after the owner forgets them a new buy works). Residual: with the registry full of junk the executor's copy-trade entries of new tokens fail until the owner calls `forgetLot` (an UI "clean up" action); the junk cost the victim real HYPE (each >= 0.001, hourly cap 17.5). Until the walk completes the budget base may include cost of lots the owner moved out (budget slightly larger, never smaller).
4. **F6** -> `withdraw()` no longer credits `wdSum`; only `withdrawTo` to a non-owner non-self address does (1.3).
5. **F7 sliding windows.** Fees (30 d), buys (1 h), gas (1 d): two aligned buckets; total = current + previous * (share of the current slot still to run). Edge bursts are gone; round 2 (L-1) corrected this wording: the first version under-counted spend concentrated at the end of two adjacent slots (up to 2x the limit inside one span, not "< 1 wei" of slack). The total is now `max(cur + prev * w, prev)` (w = share of the current slot still to run), so one span can never carry more than the limit; the price is that after a full-cap spend in slot k nothing more is allowed until the previous bucket stops counting in full, i.e. up to two slots (the sustained rate stays at most 17.5/h; a burst needs an idle slot). Withdrawn amounts in the fee base keep the plain weighting (under-counting them is the safe side).
6. **refundGas** day total also <= 5% of (balance before the call) per account.
7. **MetaMask HybridDeleGator interface check.** The Solidity source of the delegation framework is not in this repo (`lib/` has only forge-std); the published ABI (`@metamask/delegation-abis` 2.0.0, `HybridDeleGator`) was inspected: `owner() view returns (address)` (the EOA signer; Ownable: `transferOwnership`, `renounceOwnership`), `NAME()`, `getKey`, `getKeyIdHashes` (passkeys), `execute(...)` (EntryPoint/self only), `executeFromExecutor(bytes32,bytes)` (DelegationManager). So `ownerOf` reading `owner()` is the right selector 0x8da5cb5b with an address return; the e2e on the real deployed Hybrid account confirms the gate reads the client's wallet. Caveats: `owner()` is `address(0)` after `renounceOwnership` and may be a non-EOA after `transferOwnership` (the gate then reverts `NoBeneficiary`: withdraw fails safe, `withdrawTo` is the fallback); a passkey-only signer does not appear in `owner()`.

## 5. Round 2: fixes for external audit job 1205 (report `audit_1205.md`; tests in `test/Round2.t.sol`)
1. **H-1 per-account reference.** `ref[account][token]`, written only by `msg.sender`'s own `buy()` / executor `sell()`; a third party calling `buy()` only writes its own key, so nobody can poison or block another account. The first note for a lot is the account's own buy price; the step cap uses the FADED value (`_refPrice * 1.5`), never the stored undecayed price; a note with no live reference may not exceed 1.5x the lot's cost-basis price (`ethIn / tokens`). An attacker can still poison its own account; that is its own loss. Tests: `test_poison_attacker_account_does_not_touch_victim_reference` (the auditor's `test_poison`: the victim sells at t+0 and t+1.9 days), `test_third_party_buy_writes_only_its_own_key`, `test_faded_step_rule`.
2. **M-1 profit leak through a rigged route: documented residual, no new on-chain cap (business rule).** Executor sells must be able to exit a whole position at once when the copied trader exits, so `sell()` has NO count, fraction or proceeds cap. On-chain: a sale below the lot's cost books the shortfall; a sale below 70% of the account's reference also books that shortfall (the larger of the two); both count against the 30% / 7-day budget. A profit leak on an appreciated lot through a rigged route therefore needs the STOLEN EXECUTOR KEY and is mitigated off-chain: the bridge / guard watcher compares every executor sale price with the market and the guardian pauses executor sells (`setPaused`); the client's `exitSell` and `withdraw` are never affected by the pause. Not closed on-chain; stated as such to the owner.
3. **L-1 conservative window.** `_weigh` returns `max(cur + prev*w, prev)` for buys, fees and gas; `test_window2x_buys_now_refused` (the auditor's `test_window2x`: 17.5 + 17.4 inside one hour is refused), `test_window2x_fees_now_refused`, `test_window2x_gas_now_refused`. Section 4.5 reworded above.
4. **L-2 wide price.** Prices are uint256 wei per `PRICE_SCALE` = 1e30 raw units (no 2^96 cut-off); tests with 8- and 6-decimal tokens at about 1e12 and 3e12 wei per raw unit get a reference and the 70% floor applies.
5. **L-3.** `_sale` always calls `_trim(msg.sender, token)` for the sold token before the lot check, independent of the gas-bounded cursor walk; test with 31 gas-burning junk lots and tokens moved out by the owner.
6. **L-4.** `_roll` keeps the stored bucket when `bucket >= now/span`; `_feeRoll` uses `nb = max(now/span, stored)`; with a stored bucket ahead of the clock the previous bucket counts in full. Tests for buys and fees.
7. Unchanged and re-checked: `withdraw()` of the full balance to the owner even when paused; `exitSell` / `exit` never capped; fee <= 20% per call and per 30 days; buy cap 17.5 HYPE/hour. Runtime size 22,089 bytes (limit 24,576).
