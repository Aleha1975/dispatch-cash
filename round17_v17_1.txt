Audit scope — round 17 (GuardedSwap v17.1)

What to review: src/GuardedSwap.sol on branch v17.1, only the changes from v17 (commit 1dbb354, the round-16 subject, job 1063: 0 Critical, 0 High, 0 Medium, 2 Low, 4 Informational) and their seams with the rest of the contract. One contract, no dependencies. Not deployed; v15 0x2c894b3a726b49d9e68e71d548157964e5e0fbb6 is live on Base.

Changes v17 -> v17.1
1. L-1 (reconcile on a transient reading). reconcile(account) is no longer open to anyone: it reverts NotReconciler unless msg.sender is the account itself, the guardian or EXIT_KEY. The account's own UI batches transfer + reconcile; the watcher calls it with the guardian key. The router's EXECUTOR and any other address cannot. Lowering and closing semantics, the Reconciled event and the global lock are unchanged; the reconciliation inside sale/exit/buy is unchanged and still permissionless in effect (it runs for msg.sender).
2. L-2 (balance read gas). _balanceOf reverts GasTooLow() when gasleft() < GAS_FLOOR (102 000 = BALANCE_GAS * 64 / 63 plus margin) before it issues the staticcall, so a read always receives its full BALANCE_GAS allowance or the whole call reverts; a low caller-chosen gas limit can no longer make an honest-looking token answer 0 through an inner try/catch. Applies to every use: buy measurement, _trim/_trimmed, _settle, _sweepToken and the views (budgetOf, floorFor, lotOf quote with the same helper; an eth_call needs the usual gas).
3. I-3. _balanceOf masks the address argument to 160 bits (defence in depth).
4. I-4(a). The guardian-rotation NatSpec moved from replaceGuardian to proposeGuardian.
Not changed: I-1 (aliased entry points), I-2 (pull-side fee), I-4(b),(c) are accepted as informational; storage layout, budget, pause, unlock, exit, routes, hourly limits are as in v17.

Claims to attack
D1. Only the account, the guardian and EXIT_KEY can run reconcile(account); no other caller, contract or delegation chain can lower or close a lot except through the account's own sale/exit/buy.
D2. No gas limit chosen by a caller can make any _balanceOf call run with less than BALANCE_GAS: either the staticcall gets min(BALANCE_GAS, 63/64 of the rest) = BALANCE_GAS, or the transaction reverts with GasTooLow. Check the 63/64 arithmetic, the cost of the opcodes between the gasleft() check and the staticcall (cold address access included), and EIP-150 behaviour.
D3. GAS_FLOOR cannot block a sale or exit for a caller who supplies a normal gas limit: a walk over 32 lots that each burn the whole allowance needs roughly 3.5M gas and nothing else; eth_estimateGas finds it. Check that GasTooLow cannot be triggered by a token inside the walk (a token cannot lower the gas the next read sees below the floor by anything other than burning its own allowance, which the caller paid for).
D4. v17.1 introduces no new way to raise the base and does not change C1-C7 of round 16; the register, costOf and the quote functions are untouched.
D5. A stolen executor key (no access to the guardian or the cold key) cannot call reconcile on any account except through a delegation the account signed; the engine's executor delegations are restricted to the gate's buy/sell (+ approve to the gate).

Accepted residuals (do not re-report unless a bound does not hold): R1-R7 of round 16 unchanged. R6 now has a narrower reading: between the owner's outgoing transfer and the guardian's (or the account's own) reconcile, giving the tokens back reinstates the emptied lot's cost; the owner's UI batches reconcile with the transfer. A guardian key compromise can lower or close lots on balances that really read low (not raise them) and can already pause; accepted.
