Elysium LP smart accounts: per-client MetaMask Delegation Framework accounts managed through one module, with a custom custody caveat enforcer - audit round 5 (FIX VERIFICATION BEFORE THE PILOT: verify the round-4 fixes, hunt principal-loss paths only)

CONTEXT FOR THIS ROUND
Round 4 (two reports, jobs 1240 and 1241) found one High and five Mediums. Both reports agree that no third party can move
funds outside {client, pool, hedgeTreasury, feeRecipient} and that no one can block a client exit. Three findings did
reach principal: H-1 (a fee charged on principal after the client empties and refills a tracked position), M-1 (the
stolen-keeper bound is measured against an anchor the keeper walks the full refDriftBps in one report) and 1241 M-3
(keeper + guardian inflate hedge equity, which widens every value-relative budget and lets the keeper pull treasury
USDC). All are fixed in this submission. The pilot is still about USD 250 of the operator's own funds in one or two
accounts on Elysium mainnet. For this round, please:
- VERIFY the round-4 fixes (list below) against the two round-4 reports, ESPECIALLY H-1, M-1 and 1241 M-3;
- HUNT only for paths where client PRINCIPAL can be lost or moved: funds leaving the account to an address other than the
  client, the pool, the immutable hedge treasury (bounded) or the fee recipient (bounded); a fee charged on principal
  beyond the stated bounds; a client unable to exit or to stop the module; a stolen keeper exceeding the stated bound.
- Fee-accuracy residuals that only LOWER the operator's fee are ACCEPTED (listed in the response); please do not spend
  effort on them, except to say if one of them is in fact a principal-loss path. Several fixes deliberately trade fee
  accuracy for safety (e.g. fees of a position the client changed are not counted as yield).

TRUST MODEL AND ROLES (unchanged)
client: owns the account, every token and position NFT; acts directly at any time; revoke() stops management. module owner
= VaultTimelock (>= 24 h, a Safe as proposer / canceller). keeper (hot key): every module action within on-chain budgets.
guardian: pause, one-shot price ranges and hedge approvals. relayer: deploys accounts, first registration and onboard
(permissionless). hedgeTreasury / feeRecipient: immutable operator addresses.
