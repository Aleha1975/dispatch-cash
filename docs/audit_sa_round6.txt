CONTEXT FOR THIS ROUND
Round 5 (job 1249) found no Critical or High and four Mediums. All four reached principal in bounded ways. The report
agrees that no third party can move funds outside {client, pool, hedgeTreasury, feeRecipient} and that no one can block
a client exit. The four Mediums:
- M-1: a fee on principal after the client dips and restores a tracked position.
- M-2: the stolen-keeper bound grew by refDriftBps per unpaused day.
- M-3: the fee gate's trailing low followed a walked anchor.
- M-4: a flow pending through an untrusted period was booked at a later price.
All four are fixed in this submission, together with the four Lows and one Info (I-1). The pilot is still about USD 250
of the operator's own funds in one or two accounts on Elysium mainnet. For this round, please:
- VERIFY the round-5 fixes (list below) against the round-5 report, ESPECIALLY M-1 and M-2;
- HUNT only for paths where client PRINCIPAL can be lost or moved:
  - funds leaving the account to an address other than the client, the pool, the immutable hedge treasury (bounded) or
    the fee recipient (bounded);
  - a fee charged on principal beyond the stated bounds;
  - a client unable to exit or to stop the module;
  - a stolen keeper exceeding the stated bound.
- Fee-accuracy residuals that only LOWER the operator's fee are ACCEPTED (listed in the response). Please do not spend
  effort on them, except to say if one of them is in fact a principal-loss path. Several fixes deliberately trade fee
  accuracy or liveness for safety:
  - the M-4 high-water-mark adjustment lowers the fee for any flow booked at another anchor;
  - the fee waits for the guardian's confirmation in a rally;
  - LP adds are charged 1:1.

TRUST MODEL AND ROLES (unchanged, except the guardian's confirmation duty)
- client: owns the account, every token and position NFT; acts directly at any time; revoke() stops management.
- module owner: VaultTimelock (>= 24 h, a Safe as proposer / canceller).
- keeper (hot key): every module action within on-chain budgets.
- guardian: pause, one-shot price ranges (now also the confirmed level the anchor is bound to) and hedge approvals.
- relayer: deploys accounts, first registration and onboard (permissionless).
- hedgeTreasury / feeRecipient: immutable operator addresses.
