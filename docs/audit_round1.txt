DESIGN IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Clients deposit USDC (permit or approve) into a queue and request redeems by locking shares. Only the keeper's
settle(price, hedgeEquity) mints/burns shares, at NAV = idle USDC (minus queued deposits and unpaid claims) + idle
HYPE/WHYPE and LP amounts valued at the keeper price + reported hedge equity. Settle reverts unless the keeper price
is within poolBandBps (1.5%) of the pool price. Redeem USDC is pushed; failed pushes become claimable. 15% fee on
pps gain above a non-decreasing HWM, minted as shares. 6-decimal virtual-share offset.

TRUST MODEL AND ROLES
- owner = VaultTimelock (48 h default, hard floor 24 h, delay only changeable through itself, no admin). Operator
  multisig is proposer and executor; operator and guardian can cancel; the guardian cannot cancel operations that
  target the timelock itself (so it cannot veto its own rotation). Owner can set keeper, guardian, fee recipient,
  params within hard caps, unpause. Owner can never move funds.
- keeper (hot key): settle; mint/increase/decrease/burn positions in the fixed pool; exact-input swaps through the
  fixed pool with min-out from its price; send USDC margin only to the immutable hedgeTreasury; pull it back.
  Turnover <= 2x NAV per rolling day; keeper price within band of pool and within 10% of last settled price.
- guardian: instant pause (deposits, mints, increases, swaps, hedge sends; never redeems/settle/claims/cancels);
  co-confirms one exact hedge equity value per epoch when the drift exceeds 5% of NAV per rolling day.
- hedgeTreasury: operator-controlled address bridging to a Hyperliquid account that holds the HYPE short. Up to
  hedgeCapBps (35%, hard cap 50%) of NAV is under operator custody off-chain by design.
- anyone: emergencyUnwind after 72 h without settlement (10% price band until day 7, then unconditional), then
  pro-rata in-kind emergencyRedeem.

OUT OF SCOPE (INTENTIONAL)
- Keeper honesty within the on-chain bounds (price inside the band, hedge equity inside the daily budget, swap
  timing): we want to know if the bounds are wrong or bypassable, not that a keeper can act badly inside them.
- Hyperliquid, the bridge, the treasury's off-chain custody and the HL short itself.
- Uniswap v4, Permit2, bridged USDC, keeper/ bot and web/ page.

QUESTIONS FOR THE AUDITOR
1. Band-price extraction: with the keeper able to pick any price inside poolBandBps and an accomplice queueing
   deposits/redeems, what is the realistic per-settlement extraction, and can pool manipulation by a third party
   push settlement, mint or swap outside what the band check assumes?
2. Hedge equity budget: is the rolling-day absolute drift budget (5% of lastNav) plus the guardian's exact-value
   override sound? Any way to compound it (window reset, approval reuse, interplay with sendHedgeMargin /
   returnHedgeMargin, fee minted on fake gains)?
3. Emergency unwind: is the 72 h / 7 d / 10% band logic safe against manipulation at trigger time, and is the
   in-kind pro-rata emergencyRedeem (incl. cancelRedeem for queued redeems, failed positions, late hedge return)
   leak-free between holders?
4. Queue FIFO: can any client or third party block, reorder or grief the deposit/redeem queues (head-of-line
   redeem, cancelled entries, blocked USDC recipients, zero-share deposits)?
5. Fee and HWM: is the fee-share formula exact and is the HWM safe across drawdown, recovery, full exit
   (supply 0 restart) and deposits/redeems in the same settlement? Rounding direction everywhere?
6. Native HYPE: reentrancy and accounting around receive(), PositionManager SWEEP of native leftovers,
   PoolManager settle/take with native, WHYPE unwrap, and native payout in emergencyRedeem.
7. Inflation / donation: is the 1e6 virtual-share offset with settle-only minting enough, including donations of
   USDC, HYPE or WHYPE right before the first settlement or after a full exit?
8. Reserve segregation: can queued deposits or unpaid claims ever be spent by any keeper path (Permit2 sub-
   allowances, PositionManager CLOSE_CURRENCY/SWEEP, swap callback)?
9. Timelock: any path that changes a vault parameter or role without the delay, or that lets the guardian or
   operator block the other permanently?
10. V4PositionMath: correctness of amounts + uncollected fees (feeGrowthInside wraparound) vs what burn returns.
