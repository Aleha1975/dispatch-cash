DESIGN IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Each client owns a smart account: a HybridDeleGator proxy owned by its EOA (deployed by our relayer) or its EOA upgraded
with EIP-7702 to the MetaMask delegator. The client signs ONE root delegation account -> LPManagerModule with two caveats:
ElysiumCustodyEnforcer(terms = module, hedgeTreasury, feeRecipient) and TimestampEnforcer(expiry). The keeper calls the
module; the module builds single-call executions (approvals, PositionManager actions, SwapExecutor swaps, USDC transfers to
the hedge treasury / fee recipient) and redeems them via DelegationManager.redeemDelegations. The module measures client
contributions on-chain (any balance or position-liquidity change between two module actions is a client flow), values the
account at a global price anchor, and charges 15% of profit above each account's HWM. Hedge margin (<= 35% of account value)
goes to an operator treasury that funds ONE Hyperliquid short for all clients; an off-chain ledger splits it, and the
module only accepts per-account hedge-equity decreases from the keeper (increases need the guardian). The client exits with
one transaction (revoke the delegation + burn positions into its account); nothing in the module can block that.

TRUST MODEL AND ROLES
- client: owns the account, every token and every position NFT; can act directly and revoke at any time.
- module owner = VaultTimelock (48 h default, floor 24 h): keeper/guardian rotation, params within hard caps, unpause.
  Cannot move client funds; enforcer, treasury, fee recipient, pool, executor are immutable.
- keeper (hot key): every module action within the on-chain budgets. Never submits raw executions.
- guardian: pause (stops mints, increases, swaps, fees, anchor movement; never decreases, burns, margin moves or client
  actions), price-range and per-account hedge-equity co-signatures.
- relayer: deploys accounts and calls onboard (permissionless function).
- hedgeTreasury / feeRecipient: operator addresses; the hedge treasury approves the module so margin can be returned.
- DelegationManager owner (third party): can pause redemptions (halts management, never client exits).

FOCUS 1: ENFORCER v4 DECODING
1. Can any calldata accepted by beforeHook make PositionManager send tokens, native HYPE or a position NFT anywhere but the
   delegator, or touch a pool other than the fixed one? Consider: action codes outside the list, MSG_SENDER / ADDRESS_THIS
   sentinels, CLOSE_CURRENCY / SWEEP interplay, native value left in PositionManager, deltas from one action consumed by
   another, ownerOf races inside the same unlock, tokenIds minted in the same call, positions in foreign pools.
2. Is "canonical encoding" (re-encode and compare) complete: dirty high bits, overlapping offsets, trailing bytes, length
   mismatches between actions and params, nested bytes in hookData, the outer modifyLiquidities calldata and deadline?
3. Does abi.decode in the enforcer interpret every params blob exactly like v4-periphery's CalldataDecoder does (which reads
   calldata with assembly and does not re-encode)? Any blob both accept with different meanings is a finding.
4. Permit2 / ERC-20 approvals: can a same-block allowance survive into a later redemption, or be used by a third party in
   the same transaction (SwapExecutor callback, PositionManager unlock)? Expiration 0 semantics.
5. Stateful limits: hedge 35% per decaying day of idle USDC and fee 20% once per 7 days. Can they be reset or multiplied
   (balance inflation by a flash transfer in the same batch, several delegations for one delegator, afterHook absence)?

FOCUS 2: DELEGATION SCOPE
6. Is the module truly the only way to exercise the delegation (redeemer check, delegate check, terms binding), and can a
   second delegation by the same client, a redelegation, or a delegation with extra caveats widen what the keeper can do?
7. register / onboard: can anyone register a malicious delegation for someone else's account, redirect a permit, poison
   permissionContext, or block a client's re-registration? Account-kind detection (proxy getImplementation, 7702
   designator) and ERC-1271 signature checks.
8. Exit: is "revoke + burn" always possible in one transaction for both account kinds, including while the module or the
   DM is paused, with many positions, or with a position the keeper is about to touch in the same block?
9. EIP-7702 accounts: anything the EOA owner or a re-delegation of the EOA to another implementation can do to the
   module's bookkeeping (kind changes after registration).

FOCUS 3: FEE AND HEDGE LEDGER
10. Contribution flows (_sync/_snap): can a client, a donor or the keeper make a gain look like a contribution or a
    contribution look like a gain (fee theft or fee avoidance beyond the documented leakage)? Positions dropped when not
    owned, positions burned by the client, uncollected fees, HYPE valued at the anchor vs the pool.
11. HWM fee: exactness and rounding; interplay with hedge equity (reported value enters profit), anchor clipping, the 20%
    idle cap and the enforcer's once-per-7-days rule.
12. Hedge: cap on max(hedgeEquity, netMargin), keeper-only decreases, guardian approvals bound to account/epoch/value/expiry
    and cleared by margin moves (vault round 1 H-1, M-2, M-5). returnHedgeMargin destination after revoke/expiry.
    Can one client's hedge accounting affect another's (shared treasury allowance, shared anchor)?

THE SEVEN LENSES THAT DID NOT RUN IN VAULT ROUND 1 (please run all of them here)
Vault round 1 lost the economic-security, execution-trace, periphery, first-principles, asymmetry, boundary and flow-gap
agents (see docs/audit/round1_report_1222.md, coverage caveat). For this codebase:
- economic-security: price-band and anchor extraction per account and across accounts, sandwiching keeper swaps / LP adds
  within band + slippage, turnover budget as a loss bound, cost of manipulating the shared anchor vs gain.
- execution-trace: full traces of redeemDelegations -> beforeAllHook/beforeHook -> executeFromExecutor ->
  PositionManager/PoolManager unlock -> SwapExecutor callback; reentrancy across the module's transient guard, the DM
  and the account; multicall self-delegatecall.
- periphery: native HYPE plumbing (msg.value, SWEEP, settle with sync, refunds), Permit2 sub-allowances, EntryPoint v0.7
  zero-fee user operations, SimpleFactory/CREATE2 assumptions, the vendored interfaces vs the deployed bytecode.
- first-principles: what must hold for "tokens only ever go to the account, the pool, the treasury (<= cap) or the fee
  recipient (<= fee)"; find any path the two layers (module build + enforcer check) both miss.
- asymmetry: mint vs burn, sell vs buy, send vs return margin, register vs re-register, hybrid vs 7702, paused vs not;
  any check present on one side only.
- boundary: zero amounts, MAX_POSITIONS, uint128 liquidity, int24 ticks at the range ends, expiry exactly now, fee
  interval exactly elapsed, 35%/20% exactly reached, empty permissionContext, accounts with no code.
- flow-gap: money flows that bypass _sync/_snap bookkeeping (direct transfers in the same block as a keeper action,
  donations, positions transferred in, NFTs received from a third party, hedge margin returned to an EOA).
