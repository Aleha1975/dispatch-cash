Elysium LP smart accounts: per-client MetaMask Delegation Framework accounts managed through one module, with a custom custody caveat enforcer - audit round 9 (PRINCIPAL LOSS ONLY: verify that the fee can never be charged on principal through any client flow, the opening included)

CONTEXT FOR THIS ROUND
Round 8 (job 1256) found no Critical or High, one Medium, four Lows and twelve Informationals. As in every round before
it, the report found no path by anyone that moves funds outside {client, pool, hedgeTreasury, feeRecipient}, and no
way to block a client exit.

Round 7 replaced the late-booking fee machinery with one rule. Round 8 found two places where a price-dependent change
reached the books WITHOUT that rule:
- M-1: the opening booking at the first registration booked the holdings with no raise, so a registration at an
  anchor below the market led to a fee on principal.
- L-1: the fee credit (_feesOut) moved snapBase before the priced test, so a client base inflow up to the base fees the
  client had collected was absorbed and never treated as priced.

THIS ROUND REMOVES BOTH SPECIAL CASES INSTEAD OF ADDING PRECISION:
- There is no opening booking any more. A first registration resets the books to zero (contributed, hwm, snapshots,
  flowSeen) and books NOTHING; the module no longer syncs inside register. What the account holds is a pending inflow
  like any other, booked by the first sync at a trusted anchor under the one rule. SALib.OPEN and the `open` path are
  deleted.
- The fee credit can no longer touch base. In SABook.sync, "a price-dependent change happened" is decided on the raw
  balances before any credit: if idle base differs from snapBase in units (or a tracked position is pending), no
  credit at all is given in that sync, in either token. The credit is USDC only and never moves snapBase.
The one rule, unchanged, in SABook.sync: a booking at a trusted anchor in which idle base or any tracked position's
liquidity changed at all (in units), or which follows a flowSeen mark, sets
    hwm = max(profit after the booking, hwm + priced)
where priced = the sum of |change| of each price-dependent component valued at the booking anchor. Our argument:
- Let e be the phantom (booked profit minus profit at each flow's own price). Profit after the booking = true profit +
  e. At or above the mark no part of e is ever charged, for any number, sign or timing of flows, netting included.
- The floor hwm + priced covers e below the mark for any outflow (its value at the flow is >= 0) and for an inflow booked
  at no less than half its arrival price.
- The holdings at registration are such an inflow: the first booking sets hwm >= value of the base held at the booking
  anchor. A walked anchor (stolen keeper, about 9% at the pilot parameters) or a pushed pool at registration is well
  inside the 2x condition.
- flowSeen: a sync at an untrusted anchor that sees idle base or a tracked position differ from the books marks the
  account, so a later round trip back to the same amount is still a priced booking.
We chose this over "no HYPE at registration" because a 7702 account is the client's own EOA and pays its gas in native
HYPE, and because anyone could block a registration with 1 wei. The cost is operator fee only: an account that holds
HYPE when it comes under management pays no fee until its gain exceeds the value of that HYPE (USDC-only onboarding
costs nothing). See docs/audit/sa_round8_response.md, "Product impact".

Lows of round 8, also fixed:
- L-2: setPaused(false) sets lastPriceMoved = block.timestamp, so a frozen anchor is trusted again only minPriceStable
  after the unpause, after a new report.
- L-3: reportPrice checks the pool band only for a move (price != lastPrice or price == 0). A pure refresh keeps the
  anchor fresh while a third party holds the pool away; every consumer re-checks the pool at use.
- L-4 (compiler) is a deployment gate (build with solc >= 0.8.38 before deployment); I-8 NatSpec corrected.
Fee griefing with dust (anyone sends 1 wei of HYPE to wipe the uncharged gain) was evaluated and NOT changed: every dust
rule we found makes a client's unseen out-leg plus a small net return unpriced, which reopens round-7 M-1 for near
round trips. It stays an accepted operator-only residual.

The pilot is still about USD 250 of the operator's own funds in one or two accounts on Elysium mainnet.

TRUST MODEL AND ROLES (unchanged)
- client: owns the account, every token and position NFT; acts directly at any time; revoke() stops management.
- module owner: VaultTimelock (>= 24 h, a Safe as proposer / canceller).
- keeper (hot key): every module action within on-chain budgets; reports the price at least every 120 s; batches one
  account per multicall; marks pending flows it cannot book; charges every account before a feeBps increase executes.
- guardian: pause, one-shot price ranges (<= 2 x poolBandBps wide; a confirmation within a day of approval is needed
  before every fee) and hedge approvals.
- relayer: deploys accounts, first registration and onboard (permissionless).
- hedgeTreasury / feeRecipient: immutable operator addresses.
