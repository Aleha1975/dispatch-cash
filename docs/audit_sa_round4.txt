WHAT CHANGED (details in the response file)
- F: LP fees PoolManager paid out of a tracked position since the module's mark (feeGrowthInsideLast x booked
  liquidity) are added to the snapshot before live - snapshot is booked. Client-collected fees stay profit.
- S: the module requires the executor's record hash == keccak256 of the call it built (direction, size, minOut, limit).
- R: the keeper alone moves the anchor at most refDriftBps from every value held in the trailing 24 h (net displacement);
  a guardian range applies one clipped report and resets the window.
- T: trusted = fresh AND unchanged for minPriceStable AND re-confirmed by a report within minPriceStable AND the pool
  within BOOK_BAND_BPS (50) of the anchor. A report after a gap > minPriceStable is a move. Swaps, mints, increases need
  trusted; sync(a) is permissionless at a trusted anchor.
- P: chargeFee: trusted, sync, profit = V(anchor) - contributed, gated by SALib.feeGate (anchor <= pool and within
  poolBandBps of the trailing 24 h low).
- Lows: permit (owner, nonce) once; onboard needs a live account; revoke before registration; transferOwnership(0);
  setPaused repeat refused; isActive includes the DelegationManager pause; hedge cap-0 no-op; timelock delay cap.

TASK 1: VERIFY THE ROUND-3 FIXES
For every round-3 Medium (1226 M-1..M-4, 1227 M-1..M-7) state fixed / partly fixed / not fixed / regressed, with a trace
or a Foundry test. In particular:
1. SABook fee step: is (feeGrowthInsideLast_now - mark) x n exactly what PoolManager paid out of the position since the

   Bucket boundaries, the 27 h tail, overflow (values >= 2^112), the guardian reset.
4. Trusted gate and feeGate: is there a sequence in which the anchor used for booking or for the fee is off the pool by
   more than the stated bands, or a flow booked at a price other than the one value() uses at that instant?
