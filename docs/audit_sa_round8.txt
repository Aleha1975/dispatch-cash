Elysium LP smart accounts: per-client MetaMask Delegation Framework accounts managed through one module, with a custom custody caveat enforcer - audit round 8 (PRINCIPAL LOSS ONLY: verify that the new fee rule can never charge a fee on principal through any client flow)

CONTEXT FOR THIS ROUND
Round 7 (job 1255) found no Critical or High, one Medium, two Lows and five Informationals. As in every round before
it, the report agrees that no one can move funds outside {client, pool, hedgeTreasury, feeRecipient} and that no one
can block a client exit.

Rounds 4, 5, 6 and 7 each found a gap in the same family: a fee on principal when a client flow with a price-dependent
part is booked later than it happened.
- Round 4 added a day of waiting.
- Round 5 added a dust threshold and a raise measured from the last booking's anchor.
- Round 6 added a 24 h ring of keeper reports and a raise sized by its range.
- Round 7 showed that opposite flows net out (M-1) and that the reports are not the market (L-1).

THIS ROUND THE LATE-BOOKING FEE MACHINERY HAS BEEN REMOVED AND REPLACED BY ONE SIMPLER RULE. The following are gone:
- the 1-day wait (Account.pricedFlowTime);
- the 1% dust threshold;
- Account.bookPrice and Account.bookTime;
- the report ring PriceBudget.seen, SARange.since and SABook._late.
The rule, in SABook.sync: a booking at a trusted anchor in which idle base or any tracked position's liquidity changed
at all (in units), or which follows a flowSeen mark, sets
    hwm = max(profit after the booking, hwm + priced)
where priced = the sum of |change| of each price-dependent component valued at the booking anchor. Our argument:
- Let e be the phantom (booked profit minus profit at each flow's own price). Profit after the booking = true profit +
  e. So at or above the mark, no part of e is ever charged, for any number, sign or timing of flows, netting included.
- The floor hwm + priced covers e below the mark for any outflow (its value at the flow is >= 0) and for an inflow booked
  at no less than half its arrival price.
- flowSeen: a sync at an untrusted anchor (keeper, client, owner or any module action) that sees idle base or a tracked
  position differ from the books marks the account, so a later round trip back to the same amount is still a priced
  booking.
- A first registration at a trusted anchor opens the books in the same transaction, with no raise: that is the moment
  the holdings come under management.
The operator gives up fee: the uncharged gain at every priced booking, and up to the flow's value below the mark.
That is a deliberate, accepted trade (see docs/audit/sa_round7_response.md, "Product cost").

The pilot is still about USD 250 of the operator's own funds in one or two accounts on Elysium mainnet.

For this round, please:
- VERIFY THAT THE FEE CAN NEVER BE CHARGED ON PRINCIPAL THROUGH ANY CLIENT FLOW. Cover any number, sign, size and
  timing of flows; idle base, idle USDC and tracked positions; opposite flows; flows while the anchor is untrusted,
  paused or walked by a stolen keeper; the opening booking; a re-registration after a revoke. Either break the
  argument above with a trace or a Foundry test, or confirm it.
- Check the two residuals we state, and tell us if anything else is left:
  (a) an exact round trip to the wei that no sync sees inside one booking interval: the books are identical to an
      account that made neither flow, and only the client can start it;
  (b) below the mark, opposite flows or an inflow booked below half its arrival price.
- HUNT only for other principal-loss paths (destinations, exit liveness, stolen-keeper bound).
- Fee-accuracy residuals that only LOWER the operator's fee are ACCEPTED, including the new one: anyone can wipe the
  uncharged gain with 1 wei of HYPE. Please do not spend effort on them, except to say if one is in fact a
  principal-loss path.

TASK 1: THE FEE RULE (principal first)
1. Is profit after a booking >= true profit + e for every booking path? The paths are: keeper sync, client / owner
   sync, the sync inside every module action, chargeFee's own sync, the opening at registration, and the first booking
   after an untrusted registration or a re-registration.
   Is there a price-dependent client flow that a booking does not treat as priced? Consider:
   - a position dropped (not owned) or burned but still tracked;
   - a liquidity change whose value rounds to 0;
   - fees credited by _feesOut in the same sync;
   - the module's own effect being inexact, so that a later booking mis-attributes it.
2. The floor: is hwm + priced >= hwm + e for every outflow, and for every inflow booked at >= half its arrival price,
   for base and for liquidity, whatever the account's distance from its mark? Retry round-7 M-1 and its variants
   (partial netting, deposit first, in-account round trip, liquidity round trip), the round-6 wick, the round-6
   deposit-in-a-spike and the round-6 first booking after registration, each with the account above and below its
   mark.
3. flowSeen: can anything clear it without a priced booking? Can a client flow be pending, seen by a sync, and still
   be booked as unpriced? Is our residual (a) exactly what is left?
4. The opening: can anyone make the opening booking happen at a price far from the market? (It needs the trusted
   anchor: fresh, stable, the pool within 50 bps, and not paused.) Can hwm stay at OPEN after the registration
   transaction? Can a non-first registration take the opening path?
5. Can the rule revert a sync, a removal, a margin return or an exit? (_raise reads nav; SafeCast on priced.)

TRUST MODEL AND ROLES (unchanged)
- client: owns the account, every token and position NFT; acts directly at any time; revoke() stops management.
- module owner: VaultTimelock (>= 24 h, a Safe as proposer / canceller).
- keeper (hot key): every module action within on-chain budgets; reports the price at least every 120 s; batches one
  account per multicall; marks pending flows it cannot book.
- guardian: pause, one-shot price ranges (<= 2 x poolBandBps wide; a confirmation within a day of approval is needed
  before every fee) and hedge approvals.
- relayer: deploys accounts, first registration and onboard (permissionless).
- hedgeTreasury / feeRecipient: immutable operator addresses.
