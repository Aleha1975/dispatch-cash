Elysium LP smart accounts: per-client MetaMask Delegation Framework accounts managed through one module, with a custom custody caveat enforcer - audit round 7 (FIX VERIFICATION BEFORE THE PILOT: verify the round-6 fixes, hunt principal-loss paths only)

CONTEXT FOR THIS ROUND
Round 6 (job 1252) found no Critical or High, one Medium and four Lows. The report agrees that no one can move funds
outside {client, pool, hedgeTreasury, feeRecipient} and that no one can block a client exit. The Medium and all four
Lows were fees on principal:
- M-1: the round-5 M-4 late-booking adjustment was measured from bookPrice, so a client flow made at a third price
  (an anchor round trip while the anchor was untrusted) was booked with too small an adjustment.
- L-1: the pending principal of another tracked position served as room for a credit.
- L-2: the first booking after registration had no reference price.
- L-3: a stale confirmed level above the market let a fee be measured above the market.
- L-4: the keeper picked the confirmed level inside a guardian range of up to 10%.
All five are fixed in this submission, with I-9; the other Informationals are documented or accepted in the response.
The pilot is still about USD 250 of the operator's own funds in one or two accounts on Elysium mainnet. For this
round, please:
- VERIFY the round-6 fixes (list below) against the round-6 report, ESPECIALLY M-1;
- HUNT only for paths where client PRINCIPAL can be lost or moved:
  - funds leaving the account to an address other than the client, the pool, the immutable hedge treasury (bounded) or
    the fee recipient (bounded);
  - a fee charged on principal beyond the stated bounds;
  - a client unable to exit or to stop the module;
  - a stolen keeper exceeding the stated bound.
- Fee-accuracy residuals that only LOWER the operator's fee are ACCEPTED (listed in the response). Please do not spend
  effort on them, except to say if one of them is in fact a principal-loss path. The M-1 fix deliberately trades fee
  accuracy for safety: every priced client flow raises the high-water mark by the reported price range since the
  account's previous priced booking, and by the whole priced value after a gap longer than 24 h.

TRUST MODEL AND ROLES (unchanged, except the guardian's confirmation duty before each fee)
- client: owns the account, every token and position NFT; acts directly at any time; revoke() stops management.
- module owner: VaultTimelock (>= 24 h, a Safe as proposer / canceller).
- keeper (hot key): every module action within on-chain budgets; reports the price at least every 120 s; batches one
  account per multicall.
- guardian: pause, one-shot price ranges (<= 2 x poolBandBps wide; the confirmed level the anchor is bound to, needed
  within a day before every fee) and hedge approvals.
- relayer: deploys accounts, first registration and onboard (permissionless).
- hedgeTreasury / feeRecipient: immutable operator addresses.
